Create a NEW production-quality ultra-detailed space nebula emission texture for a Unity game, 3840x2160 landscape. Match the reference's electric cobalt blue, luminous cyan and intense fuchsia-magenta cosmic gas, dramatic saturated color and strong black-versus-light contrast. A broad asymmetrical diagonal ridge of billowing interstellar gas, with several branching fine wispy tendrils, dense minute turbulent filaments, intricate dark brown-violet dust lanes and intensely lit pink edges, embedded tiny pinprick star clusters and a few brilliant blue-white star cores. Photographic astrophotography meets polished high-budget sci-fi game art. It must have exquisite fine detail, sharp glowing filaments and a dimensional smoky body, not a soft blurred cloud, not cartoon. Occupy central 70 percent of canvas as a diagonal gas ridge, natural irregular outer edges fading gradually to PURE BLACK on all four outer margins with at least 8% black border. RGB opaque black background, NOT transparent, no alpha cutout. Internal shadows are richly textured, not large clean black holes. No planets, no moons, no asteroids, no spacecraft, no rings, no streak tunnels, no UI, no text or lettering. Avoid a flat purple haze, symmetrical designs, repeating rounded holes or big burned-out white patches. Intended for additive light compositing on black, so black is essential. Render at 3840x2160.
